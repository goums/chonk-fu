# Credits

## Animations

`public/anims/human.glb` (built by `tools/build-anims.mjs`) is a trimmed copy of the humanoid animation
libraries shipped with [Mesh2Motion](https://github.com/scottpetrovic/mesh2motion-app) by Scott Petrovic:
`human-base-animations.glb`, `human-addon-animations.glb` and `human-mocap-animations.glb`. The base
and addon clips come from [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com)' Universal Animation Library.

License: **CC0 1.0** (public domain). Mesh2Motion's art assets (models, rigs, animations) are CC0; its
code is MIT. The retargeting in `src/chonk/retarget.ts` is our own code, following the
direction-matching approach of Mesh2Motion's retargeter.
